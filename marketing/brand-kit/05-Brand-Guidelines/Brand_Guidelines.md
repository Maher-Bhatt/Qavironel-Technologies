# Qavironel Technologies — Identity System v2.0

## Creative rationale

This version moves beyond the earlier plain polygonal Q icon. The new signature is a **path-built Q**: an asymmetric open architectural loop, a separate forward exit stroke, and a fine inner route that suggests connected systems and intentional engineering. A consistent routing-line motif extends the symbol into campaign design without repeating a generic circular-grid visual.

The mark is designed as a two-tone flat vector first. It must also work as monochrome. The logo is not intended to literally illustrate every service; it is a recognisable anchor for the identity.

## Logo suite

- `qavironel-logo-horizontal-light-bg`: use on ivory/white backgrounds.
- `qavironel-logo-horizontal-dark-bg`: use on dark graphite/evergreen backgrounds.
- `qavironel-logo-stacked-*`: use in compact centered compositions.
- `qavironel-wordmark-*`: use where the icon is already present elsewhere.
- `qavironel-mark-*`: use for favicons, avatars, and small brand moments.
- monochrome logo: use when colour reproduction is limited.

The SVG files are the source masters. PNGs are ready-to-upload exports. The wordmark uses an editable font stack in SVG; the vector symbol is path-based. For a print supplier, ask them to convert live typography to outlines or retain a PDF/SVG workflow they support.

## Minimum-size checks

Use the standalone mark when the complete wordmark becomes too small. Do not compress, stretch, skew, add shadows, or change the icon/text proportions. Test at 16, 24, 32, and 48 px. The full horizontal lockup is intended for larger sizes; the standalone mark is the preferred favicon and profile image.

## Clear space

Keep a minimum clear space around the symbol equal to approximately one quarter of the mark's width. On social banners, keep high-priority text within the center safe area and away from platform crop edges.

## Platform dimensions and research notes

- LinkedIn Page logo recommended: **400 × 400 px**; Page cover recommended: **1512 × 256 px**. LinkedIn advises keeping key details away from edges because cover images can be cropped across devices. Source: https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a563309/image-specifications-for-your-linkedin-pages-and-career-pages?lang=en
- LinkedIn profile cover recommended: **1584 × 396 px**. Source: https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a568217/
- LinkedIn link-sharing image: **1200 × 627 px** (1.91:1 recommended). Source: https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a521928/image-specifications-for-your-linkedin-pages-and-career-page
- YouTube banner recommended: **2560 × 1440 px**, with a center safe area of **1235 × 338 px** at the minimum upload spec. Source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10456525?hl=en-GB
- Instagram feed creatives in this kit use a 4:5 portrait format (1080 × 1350 px); story/Reel cover designs use 1080 × 1920 px. Always preview the crop in the app before publishing.

## Photo and project-image policy

Use authentic supplied project screenshots in project-related marketing. Do not recolour genuine product UIs merely to force them into the brand palette. The Qavironel frame, typography, and layout can follow the brand system while the product itself keeps its real identity. Do not describe generated or illustrative imagery as proof of an implemented feature.

## Messaging

Primary: **Ideas. Engineered.**
Supporting: **Good ideas deserve serious engineering.**

Voice: clear, thoughtful, technically literate, direct. Avoid unsupported claims, invented client outcomes, fake metrics, fabricated testimonials, or language such as “world-leading” without evidence.

## Quick preflight checklist

1. Is the correct logo variant used on this background?
2. Does the symbol still read clearly at thumbnail size?
3. Is the company name spelt exactly `QAVIRONEL`?
4. Is the descriptor exactly `TECHNOLOGIES`?
5. Are contrast and spacing sufficient?
6. Are platform crops and safe areas checked?
7. Is project imagery genuine and used with permission?
8. Are every claim, metric, testimonial, and project status accurate?
