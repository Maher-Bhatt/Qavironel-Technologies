# Qavironel Technologies — 30-Day Marketing Plan

## Brand launch sequence

### Week 1 — Identity and positioning
1. Brand launch: “Ideas. Engineered.” Explain the studio's point of view, not just the new logo.
2. Services carousel: web systems, software, applied AI—only capabilities the team can truly deliver.
3. Founder post: why Qavironel exists and the problems the team wants to solve.

### Week 2 — Work-led proof
4. KALKI 2.0 walkthrough using a genuine recording or screenshots from the actual build.
5. Case study: one project, one problem, one implementation decision, one honest takeaway.
6. Behind the build: show a small engineering detail and explain the trade-off.

### Week 3 — Process and trust
7. “Clarity before code” post explaining discovery and scope.
8. A short before/after of an actual project improvement (only if genuine).
9. Team introduction with each member's real role and consented photo.

### Week 4 — Conversation and consistency
10. FAQ post covering scope, ownership, timelines, maintenance, and handover using the company's actual policies.
11. Portfolio roundup with direct links to real projects.
12. CTA: invite people to describe the problem they are trying to solve.

## Channel roles
- LinkedIn: company credibility, case studies, lessons, founder updates, professional connections.
- Instagram: visual project walkthroughs, carousels, short videos, design/process details.
- YouTube: product demonstrations and substantive walkthroughs.
- X: engineering notes, releases, build logs, useful observations.
- GitHub: code, changelogs, READMEs, demo links, issue hygiene.

## Content mix (starter target)
- 40% genuine project walkthroughs and case studies.
- 25% educational engineering content.
- 15% process and team.
- 10% product updates.
- 10% direct offer/CTA.

This is a starting experiment, not a proven benchmark. Review actual saves, qualified DMs, website visits, and enquiries after 4–6 weeks.

## Posting quality bar
- Every technical claim must be verifiable.
- Never use an AI-generated screen as proof of a shipped feature.
- Include one meaningful takeaway in every post.
- Prefer a real screenshot, short demo, or concrete detail over generic stock photos.
- Use one consistent logo, colour family, and voice.
- Reply genuinely to comments; do not automate fake engagement.
