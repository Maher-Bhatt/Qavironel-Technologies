# Qavironel Identity & Marketing Kit v2

This is a revised identity system, not a simple recolour of the prior kit. It introduces a bespoke path-built Q monogram, refined wordmark lockups, a routing-line graphic language, platform-specific graphics, editable SVG masters, PNG exports, and real project showcase creatives based on images found in the supplied website repository.

## Folders
- `01-Logo/SVG` — editable master logos and mark variants.
- `01-Logo/PNG` — transparent/ready-to-use raster exports and avatar tiles.
- `02-Platform-Banners/SVG` and `PNG` — LinkedIn, X, Facebook, YouTube, GitHub, and website sharing images.
- `03-Social-Creatives/SVG` and `PNG` — brand launch, services, process, CTA, carousel, story/status, and YouTube thumbnail templates.
- `04-Project-Showcases` — marketing frames featuring actual project screenshots extracted from the supplied Qavironel website ZIP; source screenshots are optimized WebP copies.
- `05-Brand-Guidelines` — logo/palette rules, research sources, platform dimension notes, and preflight checklist.
- `06-Web-Integration` — prompt to integrate the kit into the existing website.
- `07-Content-Plan` — a practical 30-day launch sequence.
- `08-Textures-and-Elements` — reusable graphic backgrounds and motifs.
- `Brand_Kit_Contact_Sheet.jpg` — contact sheet of the ready-made marketing visuals.

## Design rules
- Brand palette: Forest `#174C3A`, Deep Evergreen `#103629`, Warm Ivory `#F5F5F0`, Graphite `#191D1A`, Sage `#879B89`, Pale Sage `#E6ECE5`, Warm Grey `#737A73`, Border `#D9DED7`.
- Do not reintroduce orange/copper, blue, purple, cyan, rainbow gradients, or neon into Qavironel-owned styling.
- Do not recolour authentic client/product UIs inside screenshots.
- Never treat generated/illustrative UI as proof of a shipped feature.
- Use SVGs as master files; PNGs are exports.
- No font files are distributed. SVG wordmarks use a portable system font stack; the visual previews have been rasterized with installed fonts.

## Before publishing
Review the logo at small size, check platform cropping, confirm the production domain, verify every statement and project screenshot, and get permission for any people/client media. Platform previews vary by device.
